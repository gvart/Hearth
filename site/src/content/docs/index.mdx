<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Any device in seconds" icon="rocket">

		live status. [Get started](/getting-started/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Automations that act" icon="setting">
		YAML files in git: a device goes offline, a check fails or a metric runs hot, and Hearth restarts
		the service or calls a webhook. [Automation reference](/automations/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Adopt, don't replace" icon="puzzle">
		Plugs into the Prometheus and Kubernetes clusters you already run. Nothing to migrate.
		[Integrations](/prometheus/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Deny by default" icon="approve-check">
		Every action from the UI, the CLI or an automation passes one policy engine and lands in an audit
		log, and each device sets its own ceiling. [Security model](/security/).
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
